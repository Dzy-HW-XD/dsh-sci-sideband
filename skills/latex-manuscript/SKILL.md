# LaTeX review manuscript skill

Write the content as a journal-style LaTeX manuscript, not Markdown.

- Follow the Research Future-style single-column article template:
  title, abstract, keywords, numbered sections, inline figures, one reference list.
- Use precise section headings: Introduction; Materials and Methods (or Methods);
  Results and Discussion; Conclusion; Suggestions for Future Work.
- Write paragraphs, not bullet dumps.
- Use bracketed citation numbers such as [1]; do not invent references.
- Each figure must be cited in the text before its placement, and placed in the section it supports.
- Keep equations in LaTeX-compatible notation when they are essential.
- The final JSON is rendered into LaTeX; do not wrap the whole output in a LaTeX document.
