# Peer Review (K-Dense-derived)

Adapted from K-Dense Scientific Agent Skills (MIT). Full upstream skill:
https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/claude-scientific-writer/blob/main/skills/peer-review/SKILL.md

## Review standard

- Treat unpublished content as confidential.
- Review only the supplied proposal/report/evidence; do not infer missing content.
- Check question/contribution, claim–evidence alignment, methods, statistics, reproducibility,
  citations, figures/tables, limitations, ethics, and significance.
- Distinguish reporting completeness from design quality and merit.
- Every major issue needs: location, observation, evidence/criterion, why it matters, requested action.
- No fabricated citations, findings, experiments, or editorial decisions.
- No hype; no vague praise; no claim beyond the evidence.
- If a central claim is unsupported, reject or require a narrower claim.
- Human accountability remains with the user; output is a working review draft.
